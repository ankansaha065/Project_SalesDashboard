# 📊 Sales Dashboard – Power BI

## 📌 Project Overview

This project presents an interactive **Sales Dashboard built using Microsoft Power BI** to analyze sales performance, profitability, customer behavior, product categories, and payment methods.

The dashboard transforms raw sales data into meaningful business insights through interactive charts, KPIs, and visualizations.

---

## 🎯 Objectives

The main objectives of this project are to:

- Analyze overall sales and profit performance
- Identify the most profitable sub-categories
- Understand sales distribution across categories
- Analyze customer-level sales performance
- Examine payment-mode preferences
- Identify monthly profit trends
- Find profitable and loss-making periods
- Support data-driven business decisions

---

## 📊 Dashboard Visualizations

### 1. Profit by Sub-Category

This visualization compares profit generated by different sub-categories.
---

### 2. Quantity by Payment Mode

A donut chart shows the distribution of sales quantity across different payment methods.

The largest payment mode contributes approximately the total quantity, making it the most frequently used payment method.

---

### 3. Quantity and Amount by Category

This visualization shows the contribution of different product categories to the overall business.

This indicates that a small number of categories account for the majority of the business.
---

### 4. Amount by Customer

The dashboard highlights customer-level sales performance.

---

### 5. Monthly Profit Analysis

The monthly profit trend helps identify seasonal patterns and periods of strong or weak performance.

The analysis suggests significant fluctuations in profitability throughout the year.
